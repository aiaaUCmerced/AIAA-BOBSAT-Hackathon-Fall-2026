Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to integrate aspects of their simulations to provide a verdict on computation on-board. 

---

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. Using specs from a space-grade GPU ([here's one for example](https://www.moog.com/products/avionics/spacecraft-avionics/payload-processing-products/radiation-tolerant-low-power-sierra-gpu-single-board-computer.html)), estimate the power cost for running PCA on one 4096x4096x32 image. You can assume it's one vector of length 500m and that you're keeping the top 1% of principal components. Also estimate approximately how long that operation would take.
2. Using the above info, update the rudimentary estimates for the number of images per day from the Day in the Life chart. You should implement a new Computing Mode that takes into account the increased power drain from the GPU. Does introducing this GPU actually help?
3. Following that, implement [this demo](https://hanspeterschaub.info/basilisk/examples/scenarioDataDemo.html) or modify your existing demos to reflect the power generation and consumption involved in generating, processing, and transmitting data. What do you find?

---

For further consideration, the analysts can deliver the following:
1. A trade-off study for a 6U satellite with a GPU vs a 3U satellite without one. Enumerate the incurred costs and associated benefits.
2. Investigate a number of components for extra processing onboard cube satellites. Be sure to include a number of specialized processors (GPUs and TPUs), flight heritage and unflown hardware, and a range of sizes for these components
