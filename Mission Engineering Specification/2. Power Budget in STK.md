Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to produce electrical power generation and consumption analyses using Ansys STK to demonstrate the satellite's ability to sustain operations at all points of the orbit.

---

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. Animation showing the solar panels rotating to track the Sun. Why is this an important feature in solar array design?
2. Solar panel effective area report, highlighting how much surface area of the solar panels is generating power.
3. Solar panel power graph, showing how much power is generated across the simulation time.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1. Transmitter, onboard components, and sensor power consumption values modeled on STK Analysis Workbench.
2. Power budget across analysis time range, showing battery depth of discharge and energy balance.
3. Parametric analysis on power generation and consumption parameters (solar panel area, sensor/avionics power consumption, solar panel efficiency, etc.) to optimize Electrical Power System (EPS).

---
