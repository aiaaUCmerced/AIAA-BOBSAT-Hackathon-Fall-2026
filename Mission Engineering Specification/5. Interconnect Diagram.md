Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to produce an interconnect to demonstrate the how data types and information move across subsystems and units in the satellite. 


For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. A labeled diagram showing how power flows from solar panels to satellite bus. Include voltages, currents, and number of connections with each connection.
2. A labeled diagram showing how command and telemetry (CMD/TLM) travels from sensors to subsystems to the onboard computer to ground. Include data type (RF, optical, other) and data protocol (I2C, UART, RS422, etc.).
3. A unified interconnect diagram showing all of the above.


For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1.  A report on which data format fits which applications better. Explain why you chose what you chose.


Bobcat Ballistics is providing the following resources for the analysts' for their reference:

[Paper on Command & Data Handling Subsystem](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1564&context=smallsat): A paper information on the different components of a C&DH subsystem.
