Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to produce a concept of operations (CONOPS) to demonstrate a high-level overview of mission design, mission timeline, and vehicle state transitions & fault handling for their satellite.

---

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:

1. An experiment diagram showing how the satellite will execute commands to complete its experiment. It should be high-level commands that follow a linear flow.
2. A timeline diagram that shows the satellite’s lifetime from launch to deorbit.
3. A mode diagram that shows the satellite's defined modes and how it will transition between said modes

The CONOPS should be based on your mission objectives and criteria.

---

For further consideration, the analysts shall deliver the following:

1. At least one low-level diagram that shows software logic that allows for the transition of one experiment block to the next
2. At least one method for assessing the satellite's state of health

---

Bobcat Ballistics is providing the following resources for the analysts for their reference.

[UNP CONOPS Resources](https://universitynanosat.org/resources/nanosatellite-program-expert-area-telecons) - Look at this first. Go to Nanosatellite Program Expert Area Telecons. Then look at 5 EAT CONOPS and the Experiment plan. Look at what is defined for CONOPS and base your diagrams on the resource

[ASU Phoenix CONOPS Resource](https://phxcubesat.asu.edu/ConOps) - An example of what a CONOPS looks like from a university that has launched a CubeSat already
