Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to produce communications analyses using Ansys STK to demonstrate the satellite's ability to effectively communicate with the ground system located at UC Merced. 

---

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. An access report between the ground station located on top of UC Merced's SRE (37.36451, -120.42446, 0.037km) and the satellite.
2. Model a commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) transmitter/receiver combination.
3. A link budget between the standard satellite and ground system antennae models, using the transmitter/receiver parameters from above.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1.  Create a custom link report display the distance, azimuth, and bit error rate (BER) throughout the simulation range.
2.  A parametric study on how different distances, transceiver parameters, and azimuth affect BER.
3.  Validate link budget analysis on MATLAB/Simulink/Excel.

---
