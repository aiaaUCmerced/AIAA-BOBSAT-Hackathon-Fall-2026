Bobcat Ballistics requires that the team demonstrates their work in a culminating 10-15 minute presentation.

---

The presentation must have at least the following content (not necessarily in this order):
1. Title Slide, containing your team name and team members' names
2. Mission Overview slide(s)
3. Physical Architecture slide(s)
4. Mission Overview slides(s)
5. One slide per analysis you conducted in the detailed specification. This can coincide with one of the prior slides
