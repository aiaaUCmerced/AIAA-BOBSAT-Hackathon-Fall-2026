Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to modify the preliminary physical model using SolidWorks to demonstrate the satellite's adherence to the Cubesat Standard. This will involve assembling a 3D CAD Assembly using the provided part files and other parts generated through the proposal process. 

---

For this deliverable, the analysts shall deliver a SolidWorks assembly file that integrates the following components: Chassis, Solar Panels A-D, Power Management Array, Imager Array, C&DH array, and Communications Array. The assembly is subject to the following requirements:
1. There shall be no fixed relations in the assembly. The analysts should mate the origin of the chassis to the origin of the assembly. The assembly shall be fully defined.
2. The parts shall not clip into each other or otherwise be oriented unphysically.
3. Each component shall be rigidly fastened to the chassis using the mounting brackets on both parts.
4. The satellite must conform to the the mass, center of gravity, and dimension requirements of the [CubeSat Design Specifications]([url](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5418c831e4b0fa4ecac1bacd/t/62193b7fc9e72e0053f00910/1645820809779/CDS+REV14_1+2022-02-09.pdf)) for a 1U satellite. The relevant sections are 2.2.10, 2.2.11, and Appendix B-1 for reference.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1. An engineering drawing displaying the three standard views plus 1 isographic view of the assembly.
2. A mass budget document breaking down which components contribute to the total mass of the satellite.
3. An ADCS efficiency report evaluating the roll, pitch, and yaw rate using the provided ADCS system specifications.
