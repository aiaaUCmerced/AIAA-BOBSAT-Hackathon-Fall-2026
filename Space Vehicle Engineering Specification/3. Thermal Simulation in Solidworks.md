Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to evaluate the thermal characteristics of the satellite. 

---

Using the assembly developed in the first deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the thermal analyses..
1. A thermal simulation in SolidWorks displaying the heat generation, dissipation, and propagation characteristics for the Power Distribution Array. First, model the Power Distribution array's silver component to output 1W of heat power, no convection, and 4K ambient temperature. Then, set the heat sinks to radiate heat to ambient. Then, select everything and make sure it conducts heat to everything touching it. Save a screenshot of the steady-state thermal simulation.
2. Repeat the above but with the large silver blocks putting out 0.1W of heat power each. The board and smaller silver blocks should radiate the heat. Configure the simulation to run in transient mode, with a snapshot every 60 seconds over 10 minutes. The initial temperature can be 4K. Save the simulation as before. Does the board heat up a lot when the reaction wheels are generating heat? What heat power do they have to output for this to occur?

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1. A thermal simulation of the Assembly that takes into account heating from solar radiation and conduction between components. Be sure to estimate the heat power generation based on electrical power consumption!
2. Trade-off analysis for adding heat sinks to select electrical components.
3. Trade-off analysis for structural materials under thermal expansion and contraction.
