Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to evaluate the thermal characteristics of the satellite. 

---

Using the assembly developed in the first deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the thermal analyses. Detailed setup guides and conformity criteria are present in the Thermal Simulation Supplement (TBD).
1. A thermal simulation in SolidWorks displaying the heat generation, dissipation, and propagation characteristics of select components in the assembly.
2. A thermal simulation of the Assembly that takes into account heating from solar radiation.
3. Trade-off analysis of the effects of adding heat sinks to select electrical components.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1. An animation depicting the heat flow over time when the battery turns on.
2. Trade-off analysis for a deployable boom containing thermal radiation fins
3. Trade-off analysis for structural materials under thermal expansion and contraction.
