Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to evaluate the vibrational characteristics of the satellite to validate that the satellite can survive the launch environment. 

---

Using the assembly developed in the first deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the vibration simulations
1. A Harmonic Response analysis to evaluate the whole satellite's natural frequencies and mode shapes. This should produce frequency response plots and damping trade-off plots.
2. A Random Vibration analysis to evaluate the response of the satellite under select Power Spectral Density profiles. This should include stress and displacement plots.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:

1. Trade-off analysis for implementing damping washers to isolate vibration-sensitive components.
2. Trade-off analysis for implementing different structural materials
