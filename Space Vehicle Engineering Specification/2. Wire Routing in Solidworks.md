Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to model the electrical connections between components using SolidWorks to demonstrate the feasibility of integration. 

---

Using the assembly created in the prior deliverable, the analysts shall deliver the following:
1. Physically modeled electrical connections between components in the assembly. The electrical connections do not need to be fully defined, but they do need to avoid clipping. The analysts should make a copy of the assembly prior adding the electrical connections so that the mechanical assembly can be verified to be fully defined.
3. An interface document showing what electrical connections are made between components of the assembly. This should include the connector type, the operating voltage, and the maximum amperage/wattage of the connection. This information is available in the spec sheets of the provided components.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:
1. A Bill of Materials (BOM) containing each physical component of the satellite, connectors included.
2. A SolidWorks animation showing the order of components being loaded into or loaded out of the assembly. Why would this animation be critical to consider during design?
3. A wiring diagram document showing a flat depiction of the satellite and its wire connections.
