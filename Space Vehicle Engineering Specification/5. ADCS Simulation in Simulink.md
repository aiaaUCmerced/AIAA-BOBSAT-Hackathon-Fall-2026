Bobcat Ballistics requires the contracted team to evaluate the guidance, navigation, & control characteristics of the satellite's Attitude Determination and Control Subsystem (ADCS) to validate that the satellite can accurately determine and orient its attitude for successful mission operations. 

---

Using the mass and volume/surface area properties developed in the previous models, the analysts shall deliver the control simulations. Detailed setup guides and conformity criteria are present in the Vibration Simulation Supplement (TBD).


1. An animation of the satellite and its sensor's boresight settling over the Earth.
2. A controls analysis evaluating the satellite's settling time following separation from the launch vehicle.

---

For further consideration, the analysts should deliver the following:


1. Trade-off analysis for implementing closed loop control logic methods (PID vs LQR vs MPC)
2. Trade-off analysis for implementing different structural materials

Bobcat Ballistics is providing the following resources for the analysts' reference: 
[Simulink ACS Tutorial](https://www.mathworks.com/help/releases/R2026a/aeroblks/analyzing-spacecraft-attitude-profiles-with-satellite-scenario.html): This tutorial will help you model your initial model in Simulink. Try changing numbers to stabilize your satellite!
[PID National Instruments Article](https://www.ni.com/en/shop/labview/pid-theory-explained.html): This will help you understand how PID controls work, what P, I, and D mean, and what changing each does to your model.
[Mathworks Video on LQR Controllers](https://www.mathworks.com/videos/state-space-part-4-what-is-lqr-control-1551955957637.html): This video will help you understand what LQR controllers are and how to model them in MATLAB.
